Improve XY accuracy

Two high-impact upgrades after “XY-only joint correction”: (1) fit a small 2D measurement registration transform, and (2) fit one (or two) geometry parameters.

Can you reuse your existing 35 XY samples?

Yes, you can reuse them for both upgrades as long as:

  • the printed grid and its physical mounting did not move,
  • your mapping from “laser dot to \((x,y)\)” uses the same origin, axis directions, and units,
  • your IK branch rules / motion procedure are the same (to keep backlash state comparable).
Re-collecting is only necessary if you suspect the grid alignment, origin definition, or measurement method changed.

1) Fit a 2D registration transform (XY frame mismatch)

Even if the grid is flat and square, your \((x,y)\) measurement frame is almost never perfectly aligned to the robot base frame. A small rotation/translation mismatch can easily show up as a few mm of residual error that joint offsets/scales cannot remove.

Model

Let \((x^{pred},y^{pred})\) be the XY predicted by your FK model for sample \(i\). Instead of comparing directly to measured \((x^{meas},y^{meas})\), introduce:

\[ \begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}^{meas}_i \approx R(\psi)\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}^{pred}_i + \begin{bmatrix}t_x\\t_y\end{bmatrix} \]

where

\[ R(\psi)= \begin{bmatrix} \cos\psi & -\sin\psi\\ \sin\psi & \cos\psi \end{bmatrix} \]

Residual (XY only)

\[ r_i(P)= \begin{bmatrix} \left(R(\psi)\,X^{pred}_{i,xy}+t\right)_x - x^{meas}_i\\ \left(R(\psi)\,X^{pred}_{i,xy}+t\right)_y - y^{meas}_i \end{bmatrix} \]

Your parameter vector becomes (example, offsets-only + registration):

\[ P=[b_0,b_1,b_2,\;\psi,\;t_x,\;t_y]^T \]

Practical notes

  • \(\psi\) will be small (typically a few degrees or less).
  • \(t_x,t_y\) are in mm.
  • This often reduces “stubborn” residuals without touching robot geometry.

2) Fit geometry parameters (after joint correction)

If the remaining XY error depends on reach/posture (not just a global shift/rotation), it usually indicates geometry mismatch (effective lengths/offsets).

Recommended order (one parameter at a time)

  1. Fit \(dX\) (elbow-axis offset in the arm plane) — often large impact.
  2. If needed, fit one link length at a time: \(L_1\) or \(L_2\).
Do not unlock many geometry parameters at once; they correlate strongly with angle scales \(a\) and offsets \(b\), and LM can “trade” them off in unstable ways.

Example parameter vectors

Offsets + one geometry term:

\[ P=[b_0,b_1,b_2,\;\Delta dX]^T \]

where your FK uses \(dX' = dX + \Delta dX\).

Offsets + registration + one geometry term:

\[ P=[b_0,b_1,b_2,\;\psi,\;t_x,\;t_y,\;\Delta dX]^T \]

Data considerations

  • Geometry is best constrained when your samples cover a wide range of reach and base yaw.
  • Your existing 35 samples are likely usable, but if they cluster in one region, add more spread.
